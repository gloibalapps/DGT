GigTax

GigTax is the tax and expense tracker built for gig-economy drivers. Log your income, expenses and mileage as you work, scan receipts, and see a real-time estimate of what to set aside for tax — for Australia and the United States.

Features

• GPS & automatic drive tracking for an accurate mileage log
• AI receipt scanning
• Income & expense tracking with tax-ready reports (BAS/GST, Schedule C)
• Multi-vehicle support and a real-time tax-savings estimate
• Secure backup to your own Google Drive
