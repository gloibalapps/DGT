Delete your account

Drivers Gig Tax (GigTax) · com.driversgigtax.app · Last updated: 4 November 2026

You can delete your Drivers Gig Tax account and the data linked to it at any time. You can do it in the app, or ask us to do it if you no longer have the app.

Option 1: Delete in the app (immediate)

  • Open GigTax and tap the Profile tab.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  • Tap Delete permanently to confirm.

Your account and data are erased straight away, and you are signed out. This can't be undone.

Option 2: Ask us to delete it (no app needed)

Email support@driversgigtax.app with the subject "Delete my account". Send it from the email address you sign in with (Google, or your Apple ID / Apple private-relay address). If you can't, tell us which sign-in you used so we can verify that the account is yours.

We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What we delete

  • Your driver profile and tax settings
  • Vehicles and odometer details
  • Trips and mileage logbook entries
  • Income records
  • Expenses, including receipt images
  • Shifts and logged hours, and Uber Eats jobs
  • Login sessions
  • Google Drive connection tokens (we also revoke the app's access to your Drive)
  • Your user record (name, email, profile picture, Apple/Google identifiers)

What we keep, and why

  • Hashed trial record: a one-way hash of your sign-in email or Apple ID, kept so the 14-day free trial can't be claimed again. It doesn't contain your email and can't be reversed.
  • Your Google Drive backups: files in your own Google Drive belong to you. We can't delete them, so remove the "GigTax Backups" folder yourself if you want them gone.
  • Purchase records: Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat keep subscription and purchase records under their own policies. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
  • Website waitlist: if you joined the waitlist on this website, that email is stored separately and isn't removed by in-app deletion. Ask us in your deletion email and we'll remove it too.
  • Server backups: copies in our hosting provider's routine database backups are overwritten on its backup schedule.

For more detail, see our Privacy Policy.
