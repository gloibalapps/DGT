Questions

FAQ

Who is GigTax for?

Rideshare and delivery drivers — Uber, DoorDash, Uber Eats, Menulog, Amazon Flex, Lyft, DiDi and more — in Australia and the United States.

How does the mileage logbook work?

Start a GPS shift and GigTax records your drive, then you tag it Business or Private. Your business-use % is calculated from real trips, and you can add odometer readings for an audit-proof total.

Does it handle GST / BAS and Schedule C?

Yes. GigTax applies your country's rules automatically — GST/BAS and logbook for Australia; Schedule C, standard-mileage and self-employment tax for the US — and exports a CSV for your accountant.

Is this tax advice?

No. GigTax gives general estimates to help you stay organised. Always confirm your figures with a registered tax professional before you lodge or file.
