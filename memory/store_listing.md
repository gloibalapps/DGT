## Full description (max 4000 chars)
GigTax is the tax and earnings app built specifically for rideshare and delivery drivers. Uber, DoorDash, Uber Eats, Menulog, Amazon Flex, Lyft, DiDi — whatever you drive for, GigTax turns your work into the income, mileage and tax numbers you actually need.

Stop dreading tax time. GigTax tracks every dollar in and out, logs your kilometres/miles automatically, and shows you exactly how much to set aside — so you never get a surprise bill.

WHY DRIVERS LOVE GIGTAX

• Know your real earnings — see net income for Today, this financial year, or all-time, with income and expenses side by side.

• Automatic GPS mileage logbook — start a shift and GigTax records your drive, then you tag it Business or Private. A real logbook, not a guess.

• Snap & scan receipts — capture an expense in seconds; GigTax reads the amount and merchant for you.

• Claim the bigger deduction — GigTax compares the logbook method vs cents-per-km (AU) / standard mileage vs actual expenses (US) and tells you which saves you more.

• Tax-savings estimate — a running "set aside for taxes" figure so the money is ready when it's due.

• Ask GigTax (AI tax help) — ask "can I claim this?" and get plain-English answers grounded only in official ATO / IRS guidance. No guessing, no web searching.

• Multiple vehicles — track a separate logbook and deductions for each car you drive.

• Reports that lodge cleanly — BAS & GST summary (Australia) or Schedule C & estimated-tax figures (United States), plus CSV export for you or your accountant.

BUILT FOR AUSTRALIA & THE UNITED STATES
GigTax applies the right tax rules for your country automatically — GST/BAS and logbook rules for Australia, Schedule C, standard-mileage and self-employment tax for the US.

GIGTAX PRO
Start with a 7-day free trial, then continue on a monthly or yearly plan to keep the full app. Manage your subscription any time.

IMPORTANT
GigTax provides general estimates to help you plan and stay organised. It is not personal tax advice. Always confirm your figures with a registered tax professional before you lodge or file.

Download GigTax and drive knowing your tax is handled.
