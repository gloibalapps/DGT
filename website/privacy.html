Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Drivers GigTax ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and protects your information when you use the Drivers GigTax mobile app and website (together, the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Financial records: income, expenses, receipts you scan, vehicle details, and tax/registration settings you enter.
  • Trips & mileage: distances and trip records you create, and location data while you actively use GPS trip tracking.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: with your permission, your device location is used only while tracking a drive, to calculate distance for your logbook. We do not sell or share your location.
  • Usage & device data: basic technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the app.
  • Subscription status: whether you have an active subscription or trial (via the app stores / RevenueCat).

2. How we use your information

  • To provide core features: income/expense tracking, mileage logbook, tax estimates and reports.
  • To scan receipts using AI and extract details (amount, date, merchant, fuel litres).
  • To back up your receipts and records to your own Google Drive, when you enable it.
  • To manage your subscription, free trial and account.
  • To improve reliability and respond to support requests.

3. Camera, photos & location permissions

The app requests camera access to scan receipts, photo library access to upload receipt images, and location access to track mileage. These permissions are used only for those features and only when you use them. You can decline or revoke them in your device settings at any time; related features will simply be unavailable.

4. Google Drive backup

If you connect Google Drive, we request the drive.file permission, which lets the app create and update only the files it creates (your "GigTax Backups" folder). We cannot see or access any other files in your Drive. You can disconnect Drive at any time from the app's Backups settings, which removes our stored access.

5. Third-party services

We use trusted third parties to deliver the Service. Your data is shared with them only as needed for the features you use:

  • Google — sign-in and (optional) Google Drive backup.
  • AI providers (OpenAI / Anthropic) — receipt image analysis and the in-app tax assistant. Images/queries are processed to return results and are not used to identify you.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play & RevenueCat — to process subscriptions and manage entitlements. Payment card details are handled by the app stores, never by us.

6. Data storage & security

Your records are stored on secure servers. Sensitive tokens (such as Google Drive access) are encrypted at rest. We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, though no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete individual records in the app at any time. To delete your entire account and associated data, use the in-app account deletion option or email us at support@driversgigtax.app. We will action deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Children

The Service is intended for adults (18+) who earn gig income. It is not directed at children.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal information. Contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Not tax advice

Drivers GigTax is a record-keeping and estimation tool based on publicly available ATO guidance. It does not provide personal tax, financial or legal advice. Always confirm with a registered tax agent.

11. Contact us

Questions about this Policy? Email support@driversgigtax.app or visit www.driversgigtax.app.
