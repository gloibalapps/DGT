Questions

Frequently asked

Which drivers is this for?

Any rideshare or delivery driver — Uber, DiDi, Ola, Bolt, Amazon Flex, DoorDash, Uber Eats, Menulog and more. If you drive to earn, it's for you.

Do I need to be good with tax or spreadsheets?

No. The app does the tracking and calculations for you and gives you clear, ATO-ready summaries you can hand straight to your accountant.

How does the mileage logbook work?

Start a GPS shift and drive — the app records your kilometres. Tag each trip business or private to build a real ATO-compliant logbook and business-use percentage.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your receipts and records back up privately to your own Google Drive, and your account is protected with secure Google sign-in.

Is this tax advice?

Drivers GigTax is a record-keeping and estimation tool based on ATO guidance. It's not personal tax advice — always confirm with a registered tax agent.
